Privacy

GATS Events – event planner for the Greater Atlanta Tamil Sangam. Last updated November 2026.

What we keep

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your RSVPs (going, maybe, can't make it, and how many guests), the volunteer roles you sign up for, and, for organizers, event checklists.
  • Your account status (waiting for approval, member, admin or owner).

What we use it for

Only to run the app: to let organizers approve members, show who is coming to an event and who has volunteered, and plan events. Other members can see your name next to your RSVPs and volunteer sign-ups. Only organizers can see email addresses.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or use it for anything outside GATS Events. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email, so the app never stores a password.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase database (supabase.com), protected so each person can only see what their role allows. Each device also keeps a copy of the latest events so the calendar opens offline.

Removing your information

Email sukshrestha@gmail.com and we will delete your account and everything linked to it.
